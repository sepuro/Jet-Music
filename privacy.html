Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Applies to jetmusic.site and the Jet Music Android application.

1. Overview and Commitment

Jet Music ("we", "our", or "the project") respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about how data is processed when you visit our documentation website (https://jetmusic.site) or use the open-source Jet Music Android application.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. The Android application is architected with a local-first model in which your playlists, listening statistics, equalizer profiles, and preferences remain stored on your device.

2. Google AdSense, Third-Party Cookies & Google DART Cookie

Our website (jetmusic.site) uses Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-1460228543841383) to display advertisements that help support hosting, domain maintenance, and open-source development.

Google AdSense & DART Cookie Disclosure: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to jetmusic.site or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies (including the Google DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or reviewing Google's official advertising policies:

  • Review how Google uses cookies in advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Manage or disable personalized ads in Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com/
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookie usage via the Digital Advertising Alliance: https://www.aboutads.info/choices/

You can also configure your web browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. Please note that disabling cookies may limit certain third-party advertising preferences.

3. Information Processed on jetmusic.site

When you browse jetmusic.site, our static hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Vercel) may automatically log standard HTTP connection metadata—such as your IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, and request timestamp—for security, DDoS mitigation, and operational reliability.

Additionally, clicking the download button queries the public GitHub Releases API (api.github.com) directly from your browser to resolve the latest official APK asset URL. No registration, account creation, or form submission is required to browse the website or download the software.

4. Data Handling in the Jet Music Android App

  • Local Storage (Room & DataStore): Playlists, favorite songs, cached lyrics, playback positions, taste profiles, and app preferences are stored locally on your Android device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes this local data.
  • Optional Network Integrations: Features such as synchronized lyrics lookup (e.g., LrcLib, KuGou), Last.fm scrobbling, Discord Rich Presence, or user-configured AI translation endpoints only communicate with external services when active or explicitly enabled in app settings.
  • OTA Release Checks: When checking for updates, the built-in updater requests release metadata from GitHub Releases without transmitting personal identifiers.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California, you have the right to know what personal data is processed, request deletion or restriction of processing, and withdraw consent for personalized advertising cookies. Because Jet Music does not maintain user accounts or a centralized user database on our own servers, cookie preferences are controlled directly through your browser and Google Ads Settings, and app data is controlled directly on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Neither jetmusic.site nor the Jet Music application is directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, AdSense compliance standards, or application features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

If you have privacy questions or requests, please reach out through our official GitHub Issues tracker or review project contact options on our About & Contact page.
